About this estimate:

This tool estimates RRSP contribution room and tax refund using simplified rules. Your official CRA RRSP deduction limit may differ and always controls how much you can deduct.

Refunds are estimated using combined federal and provincial income tax brackets for the selected year and province. CPP, EI, and all other credits and deductions beyond this RRSP deduction are not modelled here.
