Under current rules the FHSA allows up to $8,000 per year in contributions (you can adjust the annual room input if limits change), and up to $40,000 per person in lifetime contributions. Investment growth inside the FHSA does not count toward that lifetime limit. Qualifying home withdrawal is controlled separately above.

Scope note: This version does not model OAS clawback or benefits. TFSA/RRSP room uses your opening balances plus legislated January top-ups (not day-by-day CRA transaction timing). FHSA follows the annual and lifetime rules described above. If refund reinvestment is enabled, refunds are directed to TFSA first (subject to remaining TFSA room) and then to a non-registered bucket.

Assumptions shown

  • Refund default: RRSP/FHSA refund is modeled as spent unless you toggle reinvestment.
  • Room constraints: Opening TFSA room is user-entered. Opening RRSP room defaults to 18% of current taxable income (prior-year earned-income proxy) plus optional carry-forward, using the same formula as the RRSP Contribution Room calculator; edit the total to override. From simulation year 2, each January adds your specified new TFSA amount and RRSP room equal to min(18% of current taxable income, indexed dollar cap from tax data). Unused room carries forward until used; overflow goes to non-registered.
  • Returns: same net return and fee structure for all accounts; no asset-location modeling.
  • Tax rates: Contribution-year RRSP/FHSA refunds equal the progressive tax delta tax(income, deductions=0) − tax(income, deductions=RRSP+FHSA) from the shared personal-tax engine (cumulative within each tax year). Contribution-year income is modelled as employment income (CPP, EI, and Canada Employment Amount apply where the engine does). Displayed t_now is the starting next-dollar rate for information; it does not set the refund. Withdrawals still use a constant t_ret. Select the tax year above to load the matching official tables.
  • Real dollars: when enabled, growth uses a real return while contribution and room amounts stay at the figures you entered (constant in today's dollars).
  • FHSA: annual room (user input; default $8,000) and a $40,000 lifetime contribution cap are enforced; growth does not count toward the lifetime cap. Contribution carry-forward is not modeled. Qualifying first-home withdrawal is a separate toggle from FHSA eligibility.
  • Non-registered: only refund overflow can be routed to a non-registered bucket; non-registered tax drag is not modeled in this version.
