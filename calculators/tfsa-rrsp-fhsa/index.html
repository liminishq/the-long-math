    Assumptions shown

    • Refund default: RRSP/FHSA refund is modeled as spent unless you toggle reinvestment.
    • Returns: same net return and fee structure for all accounts; no asset-location modeling.
    • Tax rates: t_now can be entered manually or estimated from income/province; t_ret is user-entered and constant over the horizon.
    • Room constraints: TFSA/RRSP/FHSA available room inputs cap registered allocations in this model.
    • FHSA: annual room and current available room are modeled; lifetime FHSA caps and full carry-forward mechanics are not modeled.
    • Non-registered: taxable accounts are not modeled; this is a registered-account comparison only.
