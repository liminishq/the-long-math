Mortgage insurance

About provincial premium tax

CMHC identifies Ontario, Quebec, and Saskatchewan as applying provincial sales tax to mortgage loan insurance premiums; that tax cannot be added to the loan and is treated as cash. Quebec’s rate is 9% through 2026-12-31 and 9.975% after that date (Revenu Québec).

Standard insured maximum is 25 years. 30-year insured amortization (with surcharge) applies only under CMHC Home Start eligibility.

Blank duration = until mortgage payoff. Recurring insurance never continues after payoff.
