TFSA Over-Contribution Penalty Calculator

Work in progress. This calculator is not yet available. The full version will let you estimate CRA TFSA over-contribution penalties.

When it is ready, this tool will estimate CRA TFSA over-contribution penalties based on:

  • The excess contribution amount (how much you are over your available room)
  • The number of months the excess remains in the account
  • Common withdrawal-and-re-contribution timing mistakes (e.g. withdrawing in one year and re-contributing before January 1 of the next year)

A more detailed version may later support changing excess amounts over time (e.g. multiple contributions or partial withdrawals in different months). This page is part of our TFSA article and calculator cluster; the main content is in What Is a TFSA? and the TFSA Contribution Room Calculator.
