CCPC Income Tax Calculator

Select tax year 2025 or 2026 in Inputs (default 2026). Corporate and personal parameters load from year-specific data tables.

Educational arithmetic. Not tax advice.

This calculator estimates corporate and personal tax for Canadian Controlled Private Corporations (CCPCs), showing the combined tax burden of corporate income and distributions to shareholders.

Inputs

Used for corporate rates that changed during 2026, including Quebec's small business deduction change for taxation years beginning after April 29, 2026.
Note: Alberta and Quebec administer corporate tax separately from the CRA. Rates used in this calculator are based on official provincial sources and provided for arithmetic comparison only.
2025 rate timing: Nova Scotia (April 1, 2025) and Prince Edward Island (July 1, 2025) changed some corporate rates mid-year. This calculator applies the post-change rates to the entire 2025 tax year and does not prorate by days. A calendar-year corporation would use a day-count blend under CRA rules.
Total revenue before owner salary; salary is entered below and deducted for corporate taxable income.
Deductible business expenses excluding shareholder salary and employer CPP (those follow from the salary fields and payroll rules).

Important Note:

This calculator provides estimates based on simplified assumptions. Salary and dividends are not limited to this year’s modeled corporate cash; amounts above current-year income or after-tax cash are treated as a hypothetical funded from resources not modeled here. Eligible-dividend treatment is a user assumption (GRIP is not tracked). The calculator does not account for associated corporations, passive income rules, or RDTOH. Always confirm final numbers with your accountant.
